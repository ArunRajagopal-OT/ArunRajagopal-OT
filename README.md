## About me

I work at the intersection of industrial engineering, cybersecurity and business risk. My background spans more than 25 years across industrial automation, control systems, engineering leadership and cybersecurity.

From PLC, SCADA, DCS and safety systems to security architecture and programme leadership, I help organisations turn cyber risk into practical engineering decisions. My focus is on security that supports safety, availability and operational continuity.
